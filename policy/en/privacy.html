5. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information.

In the Game, tap Settings → Profile · Account → Delete Account & Data to delete your account and server data immediately. If you cannot use the app, email the address below. We will process your request within 10 days after verifying your identity.

You may also exercise your rights through a legal guardian or an authorized representative; in that case, a power of attorney must be submitted. For information whose access or deletion is restricted by law (e.g., payment records within the statutory retention period), we will tell you the reason.

How to delete your account and which data is deleted or kept is also explained on the account deletion page.

Deleted data and purchased items cannot be restored.

6. Children's personal information

The Game does not process personal information that requires consent; it processes only the minimum information needed to run accounts, in order to perform the contract. We do not separately collect information to check whether a user is a child.

If we begin processing that requires consent in the future, we will obtain the consent of the legal guardian for users under 14. A legal guardian may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of a child's personal information at the contact below. For behavioral information related to ads, see section 7 below.

7. Automatic collection tools and behavioral information

The Game does not use web cookies. Progress data is stored in storage on your device and on the server.

When you watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob collects your advertising ID (ADID) and device information, and Google may use them to show ads tailored to your interests (personalized ads). The Developer does not itself collect or keep behavioral information.

How to opt out: Android Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID" (on some devices, "Opt out of Ads Personalization"). You can still watch rewarded ads after deleting your advertising ID. In regions where separate consent is required by law, you can also change your choice in Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the Game.

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch them, and Google AdMob's ad policies apply. As the Game is rated for all ages, the legal guardian of a child under 14 can block personalized ads on the child's device using the method above.

8. Security measures

(Administrative) Only the representative handles personal information, following internal management rules.

(Technical) Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Server data is protected by row-level access control so that only you can see your own data. Administrative access and administrative keys are kept to a minimum and stored separately.

(Physical) Servers are kept in the data centers of a cloud provider (Supabase/AWS) with physical security in place.
