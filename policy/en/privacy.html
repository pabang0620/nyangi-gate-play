This is a translation for convenience; the Korean version prevails in case of any conflict. This policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea.

nekolab (네코랩, the "Developer") provides the mobile game "Meow Gate Defense" (냥이 성문 수비대, the "Game"). The Developer complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea (the "PIPA") and other applicable laws, and processes users' personal information as follows.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

At a glance

· If you start as a guest, we process only a randomly generated account number and your game progress. We never collect your name, phone number, address, resident registration number, or payment card details.
· If you start with Kakao or Google, we receive the member number that company sends us and, if you agree, your nickname, profile photo and email address.
· If you sign up with an ID, we receive your ID and password. The password is stored as an irreversible hash; even the Developer cannot see it.
· Other players can see your nickname, the number after it (#1234), your lead hero and skin, league tier, and scores and ranks. Your friends can also see when you last played.
· Payment records are kept for 5 years as required by law. Everything else is deleted as soon as you delete your account.
· Our servers are run by Supabase (Seoul, Korea and the United States). Payments and ads are handled by Google.
· You can delete your account and data at any time in Settings → Profile · Account → Delete account and data.

3. Destruction of information and account deletion

When the retention period ends or the purpose has been achieved, we destroy the information without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered. Information that must be kept by law is stored separately and destroyed when the period ends.

When you delete your account in the Game, the following are deleted from the server immediately: the account and sign-in information (ID, password, Kakao and Google link information), profile, progress data, score and league records, friendships and invites, raid, world boss and co-op records, mail, tradeable gear, items on sale, and trade gold with its history.

Only the following remain after account deletion. None of them carries the deleted account's nickname, sign-in information or progress data.

· Payment records (purchase token, product, time): unlinked from the account, kept for the legal period (5 years), then destroyed
· Completed trade records (gear, price, fee, time, the account identifiers of the parties): kept as the other player's proof of trade and to handle fraudulent trades, destroyed 3 years after the trade
· Records of actions taken against abuse: kept for 1 year from the action, then destroyed
· Your entry in another player's co-op room record: replaced with a blank

Progress data on your device is deleted when you delete the app.

4. Provision to third parties

The Developer does not provide or sell users' personal information to third parties, except where required by law or where an investigative agency requests it following legal procedures.

We only receive sign-in information from Kakao and Google; we do not send your game progress or payment records to the Kakao or Google sign-in services. Google Play payments and AdMob ads are handled by Google under your own relationship with Google.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information, and withdraw consent.

· You can change your nickname and lead hero in the in-game profile (once a day).
· You can change your ID and password in Settings → Profile · Account.
· You can withdraw consent for information received from Kakao in your Kakao account settings under Connected services.
· Tap Settings → Profile · Account → Delete account and data in the Game to delete your account and server data immediately.
· If you cannot use the app, email us at the address below; we will handle your request within 10 days after verifying your identity.

You may also exercise your rights through a legal guardian or an authorized representative, in which case a power of attorney must be submitted. If access or deletion is restricted by law (for example, payment records within the legal retention period), we will tell you why.

How to delete your account, and which data is deleted or kept, is also explained on the account deletion page.

Deleted data and purchased items cannot be restored. If you disagree with the automatic check of a ranking record, you may ask by email for a human review.

7. Children's personal information

If you play as a guest, with an ID or with Google, the Game does not process any personal information that requires consent; it processes only the minimum information needed to run your account, to perform the contract. We do not collect information to determine whether a user is a child.

The optional items received through Kakao sign-in (nickname, profile photo, email) require consent. Children under 14 should not agree to these items and should play as a guest or with an ID. If we learn that a child under 14 agreed without a legal guardian's consent, we will delete those items without delay. Legal guardians may request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of a child's personal information using the contact below. For behavioral information related to ads, see 8 below.

8. Automatic collection and behavioral information

The Game does not use web cookies. Progress, settings and sign-in session data are stored on your device (app storage, or the browser's local storage for the web version) and on our server. Error records are stored only on your device.

When you watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob collects your advertising ID (ADID) and device information, and Google may use them to show ads tailored to your interests (personalized ads). The Developer does not collect or store behavioral information itself.

How to opt out: Android Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID" (on some devices, "Opt out of Ads Personalization"). You can still watch rewarded ads after deleting your advertising ID. In regions where the law requires separate consent, you can also change your choice in the Game under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch them, and Google AdMob's ad policies apply. Because the Game is rated for all ages, legal guardians of children under 14 can block personalized ads on a child's device as described above.

9. Security measures

(Administrative) Only one person, the representative, processes personal information, following internal management rules.

(Technical) Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Server data is protected by row-level access control so only you can see your own data, and changes to currencies, trades and rewards are made only through functions the server validates. Passwords are stored as irreversible hashes. Kakao and Google sign-ins are accepted only after the server verifies the sign-in proof (ID token) signed by each company. Administrative access and keys are kept to a minimum and stored separately.

(Physical) Servers are kept in the data centers of a cloud provider (Supabase/AWS) with physical security.
