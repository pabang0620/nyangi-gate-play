2. Prohibited acts

You must not do any of the following. The examples below are typical cases; acts with the same purpose or effect are also prohibited.

(1) Hacking and modification
1. Altering the game program, save data, or communications, or using a modified app (modded app)
2. Manipulating memory or speed, or bypassing payment or ad verification
3. Reverse engineering the Game, or accessing the server or database by means other than those provided
4. Creating, distributing, or promoting programs or methods for the above acts

(2) Automation programs
1. Using macros, bots, auto-clickers, or other programs that play the Game without direct human control
2. The auto-battle feature provided in the Game is allowed.

(3) Exploiting errors (bugs)
1. Knowingly using a game error to obtain currency, items, records, or ranks
2. Telling others about or spreading methods to exploit errors

(4) Real-money trading and account trading
1. Exchanging game currency, items, or accounts for cash or other economic benefit, or advertising to buy or sell them
2. Selling, lending, or transferring an account to another person, or leveling an account on someone else's behalf

(5) Fraudulent payments and refunds
1. Using another person's account or payment method
2. Obtaining a refund or payment cancellation through false statements when the conditions for a refund are not met, or evading reclamation after receiving a refund (requests for withdrawal under the law and cancellation of a minor's payment are excluded)
3. Repeating payments and refunds to gain an unfair benefit, or exploiting payment errors

(6) Impersonation
Impersonating the Developer, an operator, another user, a celebrity, or an organization

(7) Inappropriate nicknames
Using in a nickname any profanity, slang, discriminatory or hateful expressions, obscene expressions, personal information, advertising, content promoting illegal acts, or content infringing the rights of others. This includes evading filters by changing letters or adding spaces.

(8) Ranking manipulation
1. Sending abnormal records to the server
2. Colluding with others to manipulate ranks or rewards
Note: Under Article 8(2) of the Terms of Service, virtual participants marked "AI" may be included when there are not enough league opponents. This is not ranking manipulation.

(9) Multi-account abuse
Creating or using multiple accounts to receive rewards repeatedly, to manipulate ranking, league, or Weekly Siege Cup results, or to evade sanctions

(10) Other acts
Interfering with the operation of the Game, or acts contrary to the law or public order

(1) If you find an error in the Game, do not use it, and report it to the email above without delay.

(2) Repeatedly using an error you know of without reporting it is treated as exploiting errors under 2(3) above.

(3) The Developer may separately reward users who report errors in good faith. Whether to reward and what to give are decided by the Developer.

5. Sanction procedure

(1) When we take an action, we notify you on the game screen or by email (if known). The notice includes the reason for the action, the type and period of the action, and how to appeal. If urgent action is needed to prevent harm to other users, or for serious violations such as hacking or payment fraud, we may act first and notify you afterwards.

(2) To prevent abuse, we may not disclose the details of the basis for our decision (such as detection methods).

(3) The period of period-based products (such as the Monthly Supply Pass) continues to run during a justified restriction. However, if use was provisionally suspended for an investigation and no violation was found, or if the action is cancelled following an appeal, we extend the period of use by the suspended period or compensate with equal value.

(4) Paid products reclaimed or made unusable because of your violation are not refunded, under Article 7 of the Terms of Service. The exception is where a refund is required by applicable law.

(1) You choose your own nickname. It is shown to other users in rankings, leagues, and the Weekly Siege Cup.

(2) Nicknames falling under 2(7) above cannot be used. Do not put personal information (real name, phone number, etc.) in your nickname.

(3) The Developer may change an inappropriate nickname to a default nickname without prior notice. Even if currency was spent on changing the nickname, it will not be returned.

(4) Nicknames that exceed the length limit or cannot be displayed properly may not be usable.

7. Appeals

(1) If you disagree with an action, you may appeal by email at the address above within 30 days of learning of the action.

(2) In your email, include your nickname, the action taken, and the reason for your appeal. If it relates to a payment, also include the order number (starting with GPA).

(3) The Developer will notify you of the result of the review within 15 days of receiving the appeal. If it takes longer, we will tell you the reason and the expected date.

(4) If the review finds that the action was wrong, we will lift it and restore the currency, items, and records reclaimed. Any other compensation is decided by the Developer.

8. Compensation for errors (bugs)

(1) If you suffer damage from an error caused by the Developer's intent or negligence, the Developer will correct the damage or compensate you in accordance with applicable laws and the Content User Protection Guidelines. Whether to give any additional compensation and what to give may be decided by the Developer, taking into account the cause and effect of the error.

(2) Payment-related errors, such as a paid product not being delivered, are handled by delivery or refund under Article 6(8) of the Terms of Service.

(3) Currency, items, records, ranks, and rewards obtained abnormally because of an error may be reclaimed or corrected. If you used them knowing it was an error, even the part already used may be reclaimed by deducting an equal value of other currency.

(4) We may not compensate for problems with your device, your own lack of care (deleting the app, not linking an account, etc.), or damage that cannot be verified. However, this does not apply where the damage was caused by the Developer's intent or negligence.

(5) To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Developer may not restore content that cannot be verified because no record of it remains.
