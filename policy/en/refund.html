You can request a refund in either of these two ways.

1. Through Google Play: Select the order in your order history in the Google Play app or on play.google.com, and request a refund. Google handles it directly under the Google Play refund policy.
2. By email: Send the following to lwh970924@gmail.com.

 · Order number (in the form GPA.0000-0000-0000-00000 on your Google Play receipt)

When we receive a request, the Developer checks the purchase record and whether the product has been used. Once a refund is decided, we ask Google Play to cancel the payment without delay (within 3 business days). When the money actually reaches you depends on Google Play and your payment method.

If we do not receive the materials needed to verify your identity or payment (order number, receipt, etc.), processing may be delayed or difficult.

3. When you can get a refund (withdrawal)

(1) You can withdraw a purchase within 7 days from the later of the purchase date and the date the product became available to you. For products that can be divided into several parts (e.g., gem bundles), you can withdraw the unused part. The restrictions in section 4 below apply.

(2) If a product was provided differently from what was displayed, advertised, or contracted, you can withdraw within 3 months of receiving it, or within 30 days of the date you learned (or could have learned) of that fact.

(3) If a product was not delivered due to a payment error or the Developer's fault, we will deliver it or give a full refund after confirmation.

The refund amount for a partial withdrawal is calculated as follows.

1. Gems used after a purchase are deemed to come first from the gems received from that product. Gems received as an additional benefit, such as the first-purchase bonus, are deemed used only after all the base gems have been used.
2. Gem products: payment amount × (unused base gems ÷ base gems). Gems received as an additional benefit are reclaimed together.
3. Monthly Supply Pass: payment amount × (gems not yet received + gems received but unused) ÷ total gems in the product (3,300)
4. Kitten Starter Pack, Growth Pass: payment amount minus the value of the items already used or the paid rewards already received. The value of items and rewards follows the basis shown in the product information.

Currency received for free (events, ad rewards, rank rewards, etc.) is not eligible for refunds.

5. Purchases by minors

(1) If a minor made a payment without the legal guardian's consent, the minor or the legal guardian may cancel it.

(2) However, under the Civil Act of Korea, it cannot be cancelled in the following cases.

 1. The minor used deception to make us believe they were an adult or had the guardian's consent
 2. The payment was made with property the legal guardian allowed the minor to dispose of within a set scope (such as an allowance)

(3) Whether a user is a minor is judged based on the device used for payment, information on the person making the payment, the name of the payment method holder, and the like. When cancellation is requested, we may ask for materials confirming that the user is a minor and that the requester is the legal guardian. Materials used for confirmation are destroyed without delay once confirmation is complete.

(4) When a minor's payment is cancelled, we claim return only within the scope of the benefit that still exists, under Article 141 of the Civil Act of Korea.

6. What is reclaimed on a refund

(1) When a refund is made, the currency, items, and effects (permanent 3× speed, ad rewards without ads, pass rewards, etc.) received from that payment are reclaimed. The same applies to refunds made through Google Play.

(2) Any part that has already been used and cannot be reclaimed is deducted from the refund amount to the extent permitted by applicable laws. Where it cannot be deducted because the full amount has already been refunded through Google Play or otherwise, we may reclaim currency you hold within the scope of the benefit gained from that payment (for cancellations of minors' payments, up to the benefit that still exists).

7. Fraudulent refunds and payment cancellations

(1) The following acts are prohibited.

 1. Obtaining a refund or payment cancellation (including cancellation through a card company or carrier) through false statements (pretending to be a minor, falsely claiming non-delivery, etc.) when the conditions for a refund are not met
 2. Moving or spending currency to evade reclamation after receiving a refund or payment cancellation
 3. Repeating payments and refunds to gain an unfair benefit

(2) Requesting a withdrawal or the cancellation of a minor's payment guaranteed by law is not a prohibited act.

(3) If an act in (1) is confirmed, we may reclaim currency and items, restrict use, or take other action under the Operation Policy.

8. Probability-based products

(1) Gear summons are probability-based products whose results are decided at random.

(2) Their types, the probability of each type, and the conditions for guaranteed acquisition are shown on the summon screen, in Settings → Drop Rates in the Game, and on the drop rate page of the policy site, as required by Article 33(2) of the Game Industry Promotion Act. Please check them before purchasing.

(3) Guarantee condition (current): if no Legendary or higher item appears within 30 summons, the 30th summon is guaranteed to be Legendary. When a Legendary or higher item appears, the count starts over. The current count is shown on the summon screen.

(4) When we change the probabilities or the guarantee conditions, we announce the changes and when they apply in advance in the Game and on the policy site. However, where this is unavoidable, such as an urgent bug fix, we announce it without delay after the cause is resolved.

(5) Summon results are decided by the displayed probabilities. Not getting the result you wanted is not a reason for a refund. Using summon tickets or gems for a summon counts as use. However, if items were provided differently from the displayed probabilities, 3(2) above applies.

(2) Paid products that are unused or have a remaining period of use are refunded in accordance with the Content User Protection Guidelines, and the Developer will keep a channel for refund requests (the email above) open for at least 30 days after the end of service.

(3) The period of use of paid products for which no period is separately set, such as "permanent" products (Kitty 3× Speed, Kitty's Blessing, etc.), lasts until the announced end date.
